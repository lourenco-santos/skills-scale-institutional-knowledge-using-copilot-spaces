# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA and testing specialists validate software quality, design testing strategies, and help confirm that work meets acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end test plans
- Validate acceptance criteria and the definition of done
- Perform manual QA for feature acceptance when needed
- Collaborate with engineering on smoke tests and release readiness checks
- Report and triage bugs with clear severity and priority guidance
- Track quality metrics and test coverage across delivery milestones

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Reduce rework and release risk
- Build confidence in the quality and reliability of delivered work

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Bug triage and prioritization discussions
- Pre-release quality assessment meetings
- Retrospective review of testing gaps and process improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Developers to validate fixes, regressions, and release readiness
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are met
- Provide quality signals to stakeholders and sponsors during milestone reviews

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors provide business context, approve project direction, and help align work with broader organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define or approve business objectives and success metrics
- Review trade-offs and provide strategic direction
- Approve project go/no-go decisions at key milestones
- Help resolve organizational blockers and cross-functional dependencies
- Receive regular status updates and communicate outcomes to broader groups

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with strategic priorities and funding decisions
- Reduce scope drift and help maintain focus on outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Kickoff and gate review meetings
- Ad-hoc escalation or issue resolution discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide business priorities and approval to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Align with Product Managers on success metrics and the value being delivered
- Work with Project Managers to review risk, budget, and delivery confidence

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads bridge product strategy and engineering execution, helping ensure technical feasibility while keeping the team aligned on customer and business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine scope, trade-offs, and sequencing
- Guide technical feasibility and architectural decisions for priority initiatives
- Identify cross-team dependencies and escalate risks early
- Mentor teams on quality standards, user value, and iterative delivery
- Support Definition of Done and implementation quality expectations

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions that support product goals
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable rework
- Provide clear technical direction during planning and execution

### Typical Communication
- Weekly syncs with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Technical design reviews and spike discussions
- Dependency and risk escalations across teams
- Code and architecture review conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to translate strategy into feasible delivery plans
- Partner with Developers to shape implementation decisions and technical trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery dependencies and milestone confidence

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads coordinate day-to-day delivery execution, support the engineering team, and help maintain sustainable pace and technical quality.

### Responsibilities
- Lead sprint planning and backlog prioritization discussions
- Facilitate daily standups and help unblock delivery teams
- Support technical mentorship, review quality, and team capability growth
- Track team capacity, velocity, and delivery risk
- Escalate team-level blockers, resource constraints, and execution risks
- Help align estimates, scope, and technical commitments with project goals

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity while maintaining a sustainable pace
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work
- Build a strong, collaborative engineering culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning meetings
- Retrospectives and capacity discussions
- 1:1 coaching and team feedback loops
- Risk and dependency conversations with PM and product partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers on delivery health, risk, and planning confidence
- Work with Product Leads to balance technical quality and business priorities
- Support Developers by clarifying technical direction and resolving dependencies

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles should be referenced consistently across project communication, planning, risk management, and execution docs.
